# Prompts del test

El mismo prompt, en inglés, para los tres modelos.

## Caso 1: tres referencias (cara, vestuario y locación)

```
Using image 1 as the identity reference (face, hair, beard, earring), image 2 as the wardrobe reference and image 3 as the location: the man from image 1 stands behind the wooden desk in the office from image 3, wearing the outfit from image 2, a white cotton lab coat worn open over a pale blue button-down shirt, a navy knit tie and charcoal wool trousers. His face stays exactly as in image 1: short skin fade with volume swept up and back on top, grey-flecked beard on the chin and jawline with a dark moustache, and the small stud earring in his left ear. The office from image 3 stays identical: venetian blinds, red leather sofa, framed diplomas, green banker's lamp. Medium shot at eye level, 35mm lens, warm late-afternoon sunlight through the blinds from the left. Cinematic photorealistic, natural skin texture with visible pores, correct anatomy with five fingers on each visible hand.
```

## Caso 2: cambio de materiales en una maqueta de Los Amigurumis

```
In image 1, change only two textiles: the round crochet rug on the floor becomes a round rug of thick felted wool in deep mustard yellow, and the cream knitted blanket inside the wicker basket becomes a chunky cable-knit blanket in brick red. Keep the camera angle, the attic room, the wooden beams, the chair, the side table, the lamp, the books, the window and the sunset light exactly as they are. The new textiles read as handmade miniature fabrics with visible fibers, lit by the same warm sunset light from the window.
```

## Caso 3: texto con tilde en una placa (Mala Praxis)

```
In image 1, add a small rectangular brass nameplate screwed to the center of the wooden door at eye height, engraved with the word "DIRECCIÓN" in black serif capital letters, spelled exactly as quoted. Keep the room, the light, the furniture, the diplomas and the camera angle exactly as they are. The plate is slightly worn and catches the warm sunlight the same way the brass door handle does.
```
